Photo Proof Upload

Attach on-site images of road, water, or sanitation issues directly with your complaint.

📍

Jurisdiction Tagging

Automatic routing to the exact Taluka & Gram Panchayat responsible for grievance redressal.

⚡

Live Status Tracking

Monitor whether your complaint is Pending, In Progress, or Resolved in real time.

Secure OTP Verification

Two-step email OTP verification ensuring verified and genuine citizen submissions.
